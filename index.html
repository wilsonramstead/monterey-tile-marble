A showroom built for real projects

Monterey Tile & Marble is a Seaside showroom stocked with a huge selection of tile and marble in every style and texture — polished and honed, classic and modern, small repair pieces and full slabs.

Homeowners, designers and contractors come in to pick out backsplashes, countertops, flooring, shower surrounds and natural-stone material for garden paths and patios. Bring your dimensions and a photo, and the team will help you find the right stone at the right price.

Whether you're remodeling one room or building a new home from the ground up, it starts with the right material — and it starts here.
